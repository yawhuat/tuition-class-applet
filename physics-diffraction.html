What is diffraction?

Diffraction is the spreading of waves when they pass through a gap or around an obstacle.

When is it strongest?

Diffraction is strongest when the gap size is close to or smaller than the wavelength.

Student activity

Reduce the gap width and observe whether the waves spread more after passing through the gap.
